## Summary for the owner

**Stories: 49 walked.** 13 pass · 16 fail (new issues) · 8 known · 3 owner-eyeball · 1 not-built · 8 skip. These totals include the 2026-11-27 re-run of the skipped journeys (§ Re-run of skipped journeys).
**New issues: 19** (#53–#71): 2 blockers · 11 major · 5 minor · 1 nit (tooling).

Blockers first:

- **#51 (known): the translation journey is unreachable.** Reproduced: after a full transcription the Languages section is permanently "Transcribe or set a source language…", and it stays that way even after an `es` track is imported from a file.
- **#55: merging caption blocks is impossible.** `⌘⇧M` / `Ctrl+⇧M` do nothing even though the shortcuts overlay lists them, and there is no menu or button for split or merge. J-02 step 4 cannot complete.
- **#71: offline transcription never works.** "Make available offline" sits at "Downloading…" with no progress for 5+ min. A model shown as "Offline ready" fails offline with a raw `ENGINE_INTERNAL`. The offline shell, project list and fonts do work. Caveat: in a fresh profile the "Offline ready" state comes from the preview server's CI seed-set (test furniture). Re-check that leg with a model the user actually cached, once the download is fixed.

Major, from the re-run:

- **#70:** reloading mid-transcription loses every row already transcribed (5 → 0), with no interrupted or resume state.
- **#69:** diarization is a silent no-op. A two-speaker file comes out all "Speaker 1", the diarization models are never fetched, and the speaker chip does nothing.
- **#68:** Align to audio fails silently (raw `ENGINE_INTERNAL`; timings stay "estimated").
- **#59 and #61 are now CONFIRMED in the harness** (comments added to both issues).

Major, first pass:

- **#53: caption files can't be imported by click.** Choose file… rejects every caption with `UNSUPPORTED_CAPTION` because production uses the unit-07 placeholder importer (`src/app/ports.ts:71`). Only drag-and-drop works, and once media is loaded no Choose file… control is left.
- **#54: caption import never shows or detects the file's language.** The field is disabled and empty.
- **#56: the overlay preview ignores style presets and `⌘\`.** Canvas pixels are identical across all 4 presets.
- **#57: reload drops your context.** Reload lands on an empty workbench (a spec gap). Reopening restores the transcript, edits and selection, but the stage is blank: no media, no transport, and no way to re-link.
- **#58: New project is broken.** The header keeps the old name, and there's no way to add media.
- **#59 (confirmed in the harness): cross-project media bleed.** After New project, a dropped m4a went into the _existing_ project (its card then read `speech-2min.m4a`). Reopened, that project's card says mp4 320×180 while its stage plays audio-only media (`videoWidth 0`).
- **#60: the status bar misreports transcription.** It shows "Idle · transcribing", a non-monotonic % (98→91→100→99), never announces completion, and ends "no model · cache empty" right after a 211 MB download.
- **#61 (confirmed in the harness): burn-in Export MP4 is silent.** The dialog closes, then nothing changes: no progress, no save prompt, no error. Observed for 95 s in the probe and 300 s in a fresh harness profile.
